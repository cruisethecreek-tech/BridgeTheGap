The privacy stance

No account. No bank login. Nothing leaves your device.

Mint, Rocket Money, Monarch, Copilot - they all run on connecting your bank and monetizing the data. Accountability structurally can't. Your money story stays in your browser, full stop.

🔒 Local-only storage 🚫 Zero tracking 📴 Works offline 🔑 Encrypted backups

So how does it stay free? Not by selling you or your data - it never sees your data. No subscription, no ads, no affiliate links pointing you at where to put your money (that would make us the exact thing this app fights). It's free and it stays free. If it earns a place in your life, you can pay once to support the build - that's the whole model, and it's the honest one for a tool that asks you to trust it.
