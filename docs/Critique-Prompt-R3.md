## What changed since round 2

**Authority (was 3/10).** Added a real, signed **"Letter from the Builder"** to the
landing (Pat Simms - husband, dad, small-business owner: "I got tired of every
budgeting app demanding my bank login and quietly making money off what it saw").
Indie-underdog, tied to the privacy stance.

**Retention (was 4/10) - two moves:**
1. The intake now **recommends the lighter "just my spending" path** by default
   (track discretionary spend vs. a daily allowance + a reward-calendar streak) -
   the low-friction habit, to fight manual-entry churn. Full zero-based is one tap away.
2. For the full-budget path, the intake is now a **complete conversational budget
   builder** - it hands the user a finished, zero-based budget so there's no
   blank-page abandonment. It covers: multiple income sources (summed), the Four
   Walls, an "everything else" expense loop (tap common categories, enter amounts),
   and a zero-based close that routes any leftover to savings/goal/buffer.

**"Deeply intimate" intake.** Between the numbers, it now asks short, skippable
**pain-point / aspiration** questions ("how's the place you live treating you?" -
love it / outgrowing / dreaming of owning; "how does the debt feel?" -
treading water / winning / starting), each with a tailored reply so the user feels
heard. And the answers **drive action**: "dreaming of owning" pre-fills the dream
goal by name; "the car payment weighs on you" personalizes the debt planner.

**The "hours of your life" mechanic made legible.** Units are now readable ("5.1
days", "33 min" - not the old ambiguous "1m"), and every figure is a **tap-to-flip
card**: front shows the time, tap flips to the dollars + the rate that derived it
("= $2,300 · your $22/hr take-home · $176 a work-day"). Show-your-work on demand.

**Navigation.** The Cover First cards and the "Do this next" cards now deep-link to
the exact category/field and focus it (they used to all dump you at the top of a tab).

**(In progress, not shipped)** An opt-in Sovereign tier: end-to-end-encrypted
"blind vault" sync (Web Crypto) + Supabase, so paid household sync never lets us see
financial data. Foundation only.
